Trucking Rate 360 Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Trucking Rate 360 processes and why. Trucking Rate 360 provides load analysis, saved load history, account settings, routing features, and subscription access. The app does not use continuous background location tracking.

Account information

  • If you create an account, Trucking Rate 360 processes your email address, Supabase user ID, and authentication/session information to create and secure your account, sync your saved data, and support account deletion.

Location

  • If you choose “Current Location”, the app uses your device location to estimate deadhead miles and route distance for calculations.
  • Location is only used when you request it for a calculation. The app does not request background location access.

Load information you enter

  • Load information entered by you may include pickup, delivery, customer, rates, mileage, and expense-related assumptions.
  • Avoid entering unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Saved history and storage

  • Saved loads, your settings, and any documents/photos you attach may be stored in Supabase (database/storage).
  • For performance and recovery, some data may also be cached locally on your device.
  • You can delete your account in Settings, which deletes your Supabase-authenticated account and associated user-owned data stored for your account.

Voice features (microphone / speech recognition)

  • If you use voice input, the app may access the microphone and use speech recognition to convert your speech to text to help fill in load fields.
  • Voice features are optional and only used when you initiate them.

Subscriptions

  • Subscriptions are handled through Apple App Store / Google Play and RevenueCat to validate purchase status and unlock features.
  • The app receives entitlement/purchase status to determine plan access while your subscription is active.

Trucking Rate 360 Fleet

  • If you subscribe to Trucking Rate 360 Fleet as an owner, the app stores driver-profile information you enter so you can invite drivers, assign loads, and filter fleet work. That may include each driver’s name, email address, access status (for example active, invite pending, inactive, or revoked), and truck/trailer numbers and types.
  • Fleet owners may attach documents related to drivers or assigned loads (for example receipts or tickets). Those files and related notes are stored with your Fleet account so the owner and the assigned driver can use them in the app.
  • Driver invites are sent by email (account invite or sign-in / password email). Trucking Rate 360 does not send push notifications (FCM/APNs) for Fleet at this time. In-app notification rows may be created inside the app (for example load assigned or updated) for the owner or driver account.
  • Assigned drivers use the email you invite to sign in and see work you assign to them. Fleet owners should only invite people they authorize to receive that information.

Third-party services

  • Supabase is used for authentication and storing user settings/saved loads/documents.
  • Google Maps services are used for maps and route/mileage calculations.
  • RevenueCat is used for subscription purchase status and entitlement validation.

Data security and retention

  • We take reasonable measures to protect your information using modern security practices and trusted infrastructure providers.
  • We retain account data while your account is active. You can delete your account and associated user-owned data using the in-app Delete Account action.
  • When you delete your account, Trucking Rate 360 deletes your associated user-owned account data. We may retain limited information only when required for legitimate legal, regulatory, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.

Contact
